S1为P1负责校验1(0001),3(0011),5(0101),7(0111);异或结果

S3 为P4负责的4(0100),5(0101),6(0110),7(0111)；
$$
\begin{aligned}
S_1=H_1\oplus H_3\oplus H_5\oplus H_7 \\
S_2=H_2\oplus H_3\oplus H_6\oplus H_7\\
S_3=H_4\oplus H_5\oplus H_6\oplus H_7
\end{aligned}
$$
