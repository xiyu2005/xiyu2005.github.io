Of course. Here is an analysis of the 2025 ICM Problem E, "Making Room for Agriculture."

This problem asks you to create a mathematical model that simulates the evolution of an ecosystem when a forest is converted into agricultural land11. The model must account for both natural ecological changes over time and the impact of human farming decisions2.

---

## Overall Goal 🎯

The central task is to develop a dynamic model that tracks the habitat change from a forest to a farm. You need to model the initial, unstable agricultural ecosystem and show how it can mature and potentially reach a new balance through the reintroduction of species and different farming practices3333.

---

## Modeling Requirements 📊

Your model needs to be developed in stages, addressing two primary areas: natural processes and human decisions.

### Natural Processes

This part focuses on establishing the initial ecosystem and its natural evolution.

- **Build a Food Web:** Create a basic food web for the new farm, including producers (crops), consumers (herbivores, pests), and predators4. This model should also incorporate the effects of seasonality based on the agricultural cycle (planting, growth, harvest)5555.
    
- **Model Chemical Impacts:** Show the effects of herbicides and pesticides on the health of plants, insect populations, and beneficial species like birds and bats6.
    
- **Incorporate Species Re-emergence:** As the surrounding "edge habitats" mature, model the reintroduction of two different native species and analyze how they impact the farm's ecosystem7777.
    

### Human Decisions

This section requires you to model the consequences of specific farming choices.

- **Chemical Removal:** Analyze the ecosystem's stability if the farmer stops using herbicides8.
    
- **Introduce Beneficial Species:** Model the introduction of bats as insectivores and pollinators to help restore balance9. You must also identify and model a
    
    _second_ beneficial species and compare its effectiveness to the bats10.
    
- **Analyze "Going Green":** Model the transition to organic farming11. Your analysis should cover the impact on pest control, crop health, biodiversity, long-term sustainability, and cost-effectiveness12.
